# PCB CNC (SRM-20) Tutorial <a href="https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1nJxkHjFghOLMLG9y3Jg8hHeLzAsGRRuC-UVfzDiuMeY/edit?usp=sharing" target="_blank">Kicad Workshop Slides</a> <video width = "100%" controls style = "margin-top: 50px;margin-bottom: 50px;"> <source src = "CNC_TUTORIAL.mp4" type = "video/mp4"> </video> ##PS70 Lab - PCB CNC (SRM-20) Tutorial This guide is a quick summary of the video above. ###0. Quick Notes * After moving the toolhead, wait 5 seconds before opening the lid. * Set the Z height to 50 before moving the toolhead. * Mill traces with 30° V-bit, then cut board outline with 1/32” endmill. * Don't clamp flutes, but also clamp a good portion of the endmill. * Don't over tighten the set screw. ###1. Turning on the CNC and preparing CCL * Press the button on the top of the machine to turn it on. Give it a second to auto-home. * Grab a sheet of copper clad laminate and apply double sided tape to one side. Avoid overlaps and kinks in the tape. * Stick the CCL into the machine. Make it square with the bed. ###2. Cutting traces * Loosen the set screw and remove the endmill from the toolhead. * Insert a 30° V-bit. Do not clamp the flutes, but avoid too much stickout. * Tighten the set screw. Not too much force is necessary. Just enough so you it doesn't move if tugged. * Remember to always close the lid before moving toolhead. * Open <a href="https://leomcelroy.com/bitrunner/" target="_blank">leomcelroy.com/bitrunner</a>. * Connect to the CNC by hitting the "Connect to Machine" button * Drag your front copper image into bitrunner. * Position your V-bit on your CCL. This will the bottom left corner of your PCB, so ensure that isn't too close to the edge of your CCL. * Lower the V-bit so that it's around 1 to 3 millimeters above the surface of the CCL. * Loosen the set screw and let the V-bit drop onto the surface of the CCL. * Retighten the set screw. * Hit "Move Job to Pointer" on bitrunner. * Generate toolpaths with the "Circuit Traces (1/64 in)" preset * Hit "Start Cut" and mill your traces! ###3. Cutting board outline * Set Z to 50. * Take out the 30° V-bit and put in the 1/32" endmill. * Find a section of uncut copper. Position your 1/32" endmill so that it's around 1 to 3 millimeters above the section. * Drop the 1/32" endmill onto the surface of the CCL. * Drag your edge cuts and hole image into bitrunner. * Generate toolpaths with the "Circuit Outline (1/32 in)" preset * If certain holes aren't being drilled through, shrink tool diameter slightly. * Hit "Start Cut" and mill your board outline. ###4. Taking your board out of the machine * Vacuum the dust. * Use the paint scraper to gently pry out your board. Avoid excessively bending the CCL. ###5. Safety * If you're ever confused with a step, stop and ask the teaching staff. * In an emergency, open the lid to shut off the machine.
