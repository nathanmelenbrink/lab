# Laser Cutter Tutorial <video width = "100%" controls style = "margin-top: 50px;margin-bottom: 50px;"> <source src = "laser-cutter-tutorial.mp4" type = "video/mp4"> </video> ##PS70 Lab - Laser Cutter Guide This guide is a quick summary of the video above ###1. Turning On the Laser Cutter * Turn the ventilation knob * Open the ventilation door * Turn the switch on the right side of the laser cutter on * Give the ventilation a few minutes to warmup and then the laser cutter is ready to go ###2. Preprocessing in Rhino * Click a file labeled Lasercut template on the Desktop of the laser cutter computer * Then go file import and then select your dxf file * Then drag and select all of the lines of your design * Then use the arrows move it into the gray box in the middle of the screen * Then with all of the lines still selected type explode into the command bar * Then click off of the design to deselect the lines * Then type SelDup into the command bar and hit backspace to delete the duplicate lines * Now if you have any additional lines feel free to delete them now * Now just select each line and put it on the layer of the action you want to do * Either ENGRAVE, SCORE, or CUT * Now type control + p to print and just hit print (DO NOT TOUCH ANY OTHER SETTINGS) ###3. Controlling Laser Cutter in UCP * Measure your stock with calipers and put it into the laser cutter * Now enter the material type and thickness into the settings * Common Stock Lab Materials in UCP * Cardboard : Natural -> MatBoard (But subtract 1.5 mm from thickness) * Plywood : Natural -> Wood -> Medium Wood -> General Medium Wood * Acrylic : Plastic -> Acrylic -> Continous Cast Acrylic * Then using the move tool, move your design to a free space on your stock * Then using the focus tool, verify your design fits on your stock * Then take out the laser cutter focus tool to calibrate the z height * Go to the z menu on the laser cutter * Move the laser cutter bed either up or down using the arrows until the lip of the tool meets the bottom of the laser cutter * Then put the tool back in the laser cutter and go back to the main menu * Then start you cut ***4. Safety * If there is issue pause the cut in UCP and get lab staff * If there is no lab staff around open the laser cutter and put the fire blanket on top of it
