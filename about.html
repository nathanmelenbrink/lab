About

The Science Center Fabrication Studio supports coursework and cross-disciplinary research. We offer access to equipment and training for fabrication skills including CNC machining, molding and casting, sensor fabrication, microcontroller programming, radio communication (internet of things), and 3D printing.

We foster self-directed learning and open collaboration. We host public events, open houses, and project fairs. Join our mailing list for events and occasional updates by sending a blank message to fabrication-lab-subscribe@lists.fas.harvard.edu.

Access

Access is limited to students enrolled in our courses. After completing the course training sequence, students receive 24/7 tap access to the fabrication studio. Access remains active while they are current Harvard affiliates and in good standing with shop policies. Exceptions may be considered with a written request from a Principal Investigator (PI). The PI should email Dr. Melenbrink with a brief description of the project and the specific equipment on which the student will require training.
