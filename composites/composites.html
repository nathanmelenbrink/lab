A composite material usually combines one component that is strong in compression (the matrix) with a second component (the fiber) that is strong in extension. The resulting material is stronger than either component alone. See below what happens to stiffness as you go from one layer (floppy) to five (much stiffer) to a cored arrangement (almost as stiff as five.) ![pic](bending.jpg) ### How it is made. ![pic](assembly.jpg) ### Modelling and exporting.
